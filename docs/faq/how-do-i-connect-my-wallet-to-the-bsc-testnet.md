### How do I connect my wallet to the BSC Testnet?

To connect a wallet such as MetaMask to the BSC Testnet:

1. Open your wallet and go to the network selection menu.
2. Choose **Add network** and enter the BSC Testnet RPC endpoint.
3. Save and switch to the new network.

Once connected, you can request test BNB from the official faucet to try transactions before deploying to mainnet.
